# Dataset sources and attribution

V2 nonsegmented Plant Seedlings: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vbookshelf/v2-plant-seedlings-dataset. Original source: https://vision.eng.au.dk/plant-seedlings-dataset/ describes approximately 960 unique plants, 12 species and multiple growth stages. V2 contains 5,539 images. License: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.

Giselsson, T. M., Dyrmann, M., Jørgensen, R. N., Jensen, P. K., and Midtiby, H. S. (2017). A Public Image Database for Benchmark of Plant Seedling Classification Algorithms. https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05458

Maize, Common Wheat and Sugar Beet are Crop (0); the other nine species are Weed (1). This task-specific mapping is not a universal agricultural definition. Controlled acquisition images do not demonstrate field performance.
